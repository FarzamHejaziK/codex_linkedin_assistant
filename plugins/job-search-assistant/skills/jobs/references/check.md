# Check Workflow

Use for jobs check or “what needs my attention?” This is local-only: no browser connection, LinkedIn verification, or resume is required. Read tracker-schema.md and relevant local outreach/application notes. Resolve the saved workspace with local-persistence.md. A missing tracker in a registered workspace is a recovery issue; it must not be reported as empty.

## Empty Tracker

Say the tracker is empty. If a resume/search preferences are ready, suggest finding the first three matches or pasting a job link. Otherwise suggest the missing local setup step. Do not show empty tables or launch discovery without a request.

## Recommended Actions

Lead with up to three distinct actionable items in a compact table:

| Job or contact | Recommended action | Why now / deadline | Blocker |
|---|---|---|---|

Prioritize known urgent job deadlines, expired or imminent referral deadlines, due follow-ups, then ready applications and other next actions. Within comparable urgency use tracker priority. Use actual known dates; label unknown deadlines instead of inventing them. Combine duplicate actions for the same job/contact. Local drafts should be offered when live work is blocked.

Summarize counts for ready applications, waiting referrals, and active applications in one sentence. If all work is waiting, say so and identify the next known check date. Do not manufacture three actions to fill the table. End with one recommended next step.

## Full Dashboard On Request

When asked for all jobs, the full dashboard, or a specific queue, show the requested non-empty queues as Markdown tables:

- Ready applications using tracker-schema.md eligibility.
- Approaching referral deadlines: pending referrals due after today through two days from today.
- Expired referral deadlines: pending referrals due on or before today.
- Stale outreach: last message at least three days ago and before the referral deadline.
- Pending connections and active outreach from contact logs.
- Applied jobs and due next actions.

Use tracker/log evidence only; local review does not imply live inbox checks. Do not mutate referral statuses just to render a dashboard; reconcile deadlines when referral/apply executes.
