# External Action Review

Prepare a concrete result before asking for approval. Use this common format for uploads, application submissions, messages, emails, connection requests, and public profile edits. An authorization already given for the exact reviewed action remains valid; do not ask again unnecessarily.

- **Action and destination:** what will happen, company/role or recipient, and exact site/account.
- **Content:** exact message or profile edit; for applications, the completed field summary with sensitive answers and any unresolved fields clearly identified.
- **Files:** links to the exact resume/attachment versions, including a concise description of tailoring changes when relevant.
- **Choices:** approve, edit, or skip. A batch may be approved only when every item and action is enumerated and reviewable.

Ask separately for unknown, sensitive, or ambiguous screening answers before completing the review. Do not assume an answer or treat elapsed time as approval.

The selected browser may require a manual upload handoff; follow browser-preflight.md and verify the resulting attachment. Neither a manual upload nor browser site-access permission authorizes a later send or submission.

An upload can expose private data before final submission: show the file and destination and obtain explicit approval before uploading. Approval to upload is not approval to submit. A review may authorize both when it explicitly covers both and the final form and files are already reviewable. If later fields, content, attachments, or the destination change materially, review the changed action again.

Prepare-only means no uploads, sends, connection requests, profile saves, or submissions. Local preparation may proceed without an approval gate.

After execution, verify a confirmation page, sent item, or other direct evidence before recording success. If the outcome is uncertain, record that uncertainty in the private session and application/contact notes; inspect the destination before retrying. Do not mark a job Applied or a message sent based only on a button click.

Approval is scoped to the reviewed action. Never treat saved session notes as reusable authorization after a restart; consult the conversation and re-review when authorization or content cannot be verified.
